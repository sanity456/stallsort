Title
MarketStallMatch - Owner-Isolated Reclaimable Stall Allocation

Notes / Description
Built MarketStallMatch, a reusable intelligent contract. Its GenLayer-native mechanism is vendor profile consensus -> bitmap compatibility -> deterministic least-surplus allocation -> vendor acceptance. Correction: Profile masks and digest are now bound to the attached payload. Applications are isolated per owner-created market, limited to one per wallet, and terminal slots are reclaimable. Inputs and source references are public, caller-supplied, and unverified. It does not authenticate products, approve vendors, allocate legal space, collect fees, or verify resource and accessibility claims. GenVM lint and strict typecheck pass; 15 direct tests and 1 five-validator integration test pass. StudioNet completed 6 successful isolated-wallet transactions. Its source and full ABI/schema match the pinned evidence exactly. The earlier deployment is superseded.
